Privacy policy

Goalbreaker, the football puzzle game. In effect from 17 September 2026.

In short: Goalbreaker has no account and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The game can send crash reports and play statistics to help us fix bugs and balance levels; sharing is off until you enable it in Settings, and you can change that choice at any time. Ads play only when you choose to watch one. We never collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Who we are

Goalbreaker is made and published by Talvero Games, the studio of its developer Doğukan Saçmaözü ("we"). Contact: dogukangamesdev@gmail.com, see also the support page.

2. Data that stays on your device

To remember where you are, the game keeps a small save file in the app's own storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Level progress, stars and unlocked chaptersTo continue where you left off
In-game coins and gems, owned kits, balls and effectsThe game's own economy
Settings: sound, music, vibration, graphics, language, the usage-data choiceTo keep your preferences
Local counters such as levels played and trophies earnedTo award trophies and pace daily rewards

This file is not sent to us. Removing the app normally removes local app storage; operating-system backups may remain. Settings → Reset progress clears the progress and keeps your preferences. Your operating system may include the app's data in its own device backup (Android's backup to your Google account, Apple's iCloud backup) according to your device settings; that backup is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

3. Data sent from the game

When the Share usage data option in Settings is on and the device is online, the game sends the following data to Google's Firebase service, which processes it on our behalf:

WhatWhyKept for
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): what the game was doing when it crashed, the app version, device model, operating system version, free memory and storage, a random installation identifier, and game context such as the current level, language and quality setting.To find and fix crashes on the devices they happen on90 days
Play statistics (Firebase Analytics): which levels are started, won or lost and with how many stars, daily rewards claimed, which screens are used, session length, the app version, device model, operating system, language, and the country derived from your IP address. Advertising identifiers are not collected for statistics.To balance level difficulty and the in-game economyPer-device retention follows the Analytics property setting; aggregated reports may remain longer

Neither kind of data contains your name, e-mail address or anything you typed. The identifiers distinguish app installations; we do not describe this data as completely anonymous. Turning the option off stops collection at once; the data already sent is deleted by Google on the schedule above, with requests handled as described in section 8.

3a. Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one, for extra help or rewards. Google AdMob and its advertising partners process your IP address and approximate location, device or installation identifiers (including the Android advertising ID where available), device information, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The SDK can initialise and preload an ad after the privacy flow permits requests, before you choose to watch a video.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks the applicable privacy requirements, including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and supported US states. Where required, the game presents the corresponding message and provides Settings → Ad privacy options to review your choice. This advertising choice is separate from the Share usage data option for Firebase. The iOS app does not request ATT permission or access IDFA.

4. Permissions

The Android app asks for vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings) and internet access (for ads and the optional reports and statistics above). It also declares the Android advertising ID permission for AdMob. Levels and local progress work offline; ads, remote reporting and external support pages need a connection.

5. Third parties

Google (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics) processes the data in section 3 for us. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing: policies.google.com/privacy. Google AdMob and its advertising partners also process the advertising data described in section 3a under their applicable policies. The usage-data switch does not turn off that separate advertising processing.

6. Children

Goalbreaker is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the game and want the data connected with that device deleted, contact us.

7. Your choices

  • Choose optional Firebase data sharing (off by default): Settings → Data & privacy → Share usage data.
  • Reset: Settings → Reset progress clears the progress on this device.
  • Request deletion: uninstall the app; then ask us to delete the crash and statistics data (section 8).

8. Contact and deletion requests

Write to dogukangamesdev@gmail.com. The game has no accounts. We will assess what identifiers are available and the deletion methods offered by the relevant provider; a device model or approximate date alone may not identify a specific installation. You can also use Google's privacy and advertising controls. Support e-mails you send us are kept only for answering you.

9. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's documentation. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date, and the change will be described in the store listing.
