Privacy Policy

Goalbreaker: Football Puzzle · Effective 9 September 2026

In one sentence: Goalbreaker does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The game works entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection.

1. Who we are

Goalbreaker is published by its developer (Doğukan Saçmaözü, "we"). Contact details are on the support page.

2. Data we do not collect

The game has no account system and no server. It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any usage analytics. It makes no network requests of its own and requests no internet permission.

3. Data stored on your device only

To remember your progress the game keeps a small amount of information in the app's own storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Level progress, stars and unlocked chaptersTo continue where you left off
In-game coins and gems, owned kits and ballsThe game's own economy (earned by playing, never bought)
Settings: sound, music, vibration, graphics quality, languageTo keep your preferences
Local counters such as levels played and trophies earnedTo award trophies; these never leave the device

This information never leaves your device through the game. Deleting the app removes it. Your operating system may include the app's data in its own device backup (for example Android's backup to your Google account) according to your device settings; that backup is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

4. Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission: vibration, used for haptic feedback on kicks, goals and buttons. It can be turned off in the game's settings. No other permission is requested.

5. Advertising, purchases and third parties

Version 1.0 contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no third-party software development kits (no analytics, no crash reporting, no ad networks). If a future version adds any of these, this policy will be updated first and the change will be described in the store listing.

6. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is rated for ages 13 and up on the stores. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

7. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
