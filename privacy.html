Privacy Policy

Goalbreaker: Football Puzzle · Effective EFFECTIVE_DATE

In short: Goalbreaker has no account and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The game can send anonymous crash reports and play statistics to help us fix bugs and balance levels; you can turn that off in Settings at any time. We never collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Who we are

Goalbreaker is published by its developer (Doğukan Saçmaözü, "we"). Contact: dogukangamesdev@gmail.com, see also the support page.

2. Data that stays on your device

To remember where you are, the game keeps a small save file in the app's own storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Level progress, stars and unlocked chaptersTo continue where you left off
In-game coins and gems, owned kits, balls and effectsThe game's own economy
Settings: sound, music, vibration, graphics, language, the usage-data choiceTo keep your preferences
Local counters such as levels played and trophies earnedTo award trophies and pace daily rewards

This file is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it; Settings → Reset progress clears the progress and keeps your preferences. Your operating system may include the app's data in its own device backup (Android's backup to your Google account, Apple's iCloud backup) according to your device settings; that backup is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

3. Data sent from the game

When the Share usage data option in Settings is on and the device is online, the game sends two kinds of anonymous data to Google's Firebase service, which processes it on our behalf:

WhatWhyKept for
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): what the game was doing when it crashed, the app version, device model, operating system version, free memory and storage, and a random installation identifier.To find and fix crashes on the devices they happen on90 days
Play statistics (Firebase Analytics): which levels are started, won or lost and with how many stars, daily rewards claimed, which screens are used, session length, the app version, device model, operating system, language, and the country derived from your IP address. Advertising identifiers are not collected for statistics.To balance level difficulty and the in-game economyTwo months for per-device data; aggregated reports longer

Neither kind of data contains your name, e-mail address or anything you typed. The installation identifier is random and is not linked to you. Turning the option off stops collection at once; the data already sent is deleted by Google on the schedule above, or sooner if you ask us (see section 8).

3a. Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example for extra balls or a hint). Ads are served by Google AdMob and its advertising partners, which receive your device's advertising identifier where you have allowed it, your IP address, an approximate location derived from it, device information and how you interact with the ad. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the game asks for your consent before any ad and lets you change it later under Settings → Ad privacy options. On iOS the game does not ask permission to track you and shows non-personalised ads.

4. Permissions

The Android app asks for vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings) and internet access (for the crash reports and statistics above). The game plays fully offline; nothing in it requires a connection.

5. Third parties

Google (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics) processes the data in section 3 for us. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing: policies.google.com/privacy. No other third-party service receives data from the game. We do not sell data.

6. Children

Goalbreaker is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the game and want the data connected with that device deleted, contact us.

7. Your choices

  • Turn data sharing off: Settings → Data & privacy → Share usage data.
  • Reset: Settings → Reset progress clears the progress on this device.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app; then ask us to delete the crash and statistics data (section 8).

8. Contact and deletion requests

Write to dogukangamesdev@gmail.com. Because the game has no accounts, tell us roughly when and on which device model you played so we can find the matching installation identifier. Support e-mails you send us are kept only for answering you.

9. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's documentation. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date, and the change will be described in the store listing.
