01Who we are

This privacy policy covers the Goalbreaker mobile game and this website. Goalbreaker is developed and published by Doğukan Saçmaözü, operating as Talvero Games, in Türkiye (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data we process for the purposes described here.

For privacy questions or requests, contact dogukangamesdev@gmail.com.

02Your game, on your device

Goalbreaker does not require a player account. It stores your level progress, stars, coins, gems, unlocked items, trophies, daily rewards and preferences in the app’s local storage. We do not upload your save file to a game server or provide our own cloud-save service.

Settings → Reset progress clears local game progress. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local storage, but your operating system may retain or restore app data through device backups. Backups are controlled by your device settings and platform provider. Resetting or uninstalling the game does not delete data already sent to the services below.

03Optional play statistics & crash reports

Share usage data is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings → Data & privacy, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to help us improve it.

Play statistics · Firebase Analytics

Information can include level starts and results, attempts, stars, screens viewed, rewards, cosmetic selections, ad reward results, session activity, app version, device and operating system information, language, an app-instance identifier and country derived from an IP address. We use it to understand how the game is played and improve difficulty and rewards. Our Firebase Analytics configuration disables advertising ID collection and advertising personalisation.

Crash reports · Firebase Crashlytics

Reports can include crash traces and error details, app version, device model and operating system, memory and storage information, installation identifiers, and game context such as the level, language and graphics setting. We use this information to diagnose and fix technical problems.

These identifiers distinguish installations; this information is not necessarily anonymous. You can turn sharing off at any time. This stops further optional collection and resets local Analytics data, but does not automatically erase information already sent. This choice is separate from advertising privacy choices.

04Rewarded ads & advertising privacy

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Videos play when you choose a reward; there are no ads between levels. Once the advertising privacy flow allows ad requests, the ad service can initialise and preload an ad before you choose to watch a video.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device and app information, app launches and ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID where available. The SDK also uses app set ID for permitted purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising data is used to deliver and measure ads, maintain the service and prevent fraud, with personalisation depending on applicable consent and settings. The game does not request precise GPS location.

Google’s User Messaging Platform manages regional advertising privacy messages, including for the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and supported US states. The message and options available depend on your region and the service’s requirements. Where required, Settings → Ad privacy options lets you review your choices. You may also use your device’s advertising controls. The current iOS implementation does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access IDFA.

Turning off Share usage data does not turn off AdMob processing. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s advertising privacy information for more about these services and controls.

05When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name shown by your email service, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Support email is handled through Gmail. The game does not send support messages automatically.

Please send only what is needed to explain the issue. We do not need your passwords, payment details or identity documents for an ordinary support request. The current version offers no in-app purchases and does not collect payment information.

06Service providers & international processing

We use Google’s Firebase services for optional analytics and crash reporting, Google AdMob and its advertising partners for ads, Gmail for support, and GitHub Pages for this website. Each receives the information needed for the relevant service. Google’s advertising partners are described in the consent interface where available.

These providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. Applicable transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses or recognised transfer frameworks where relevant, are described in their policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

07Retention & security

Different information has different retention periods:

  • Local game data: stays on your device until reset or removed, subject to device backup behaviour.
  • Crash reports: Google states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.
  • Analytics: user-level data follows the Analytics property’s configured retention period. Aggregated reports may remain longer. Turning sharing off does not itself delete past reports.
  • Advertising data: retention depends on Google and the relevant advertising partner, the purpose and applicable requirements.
  • Support messages: we retain messages as needed to answer your request and resolve related issues, or where retention is required for legal obligations or claims.

We use access controls to protect the services we manage. Firebase and Google Mobile Ads use encrypted connections for data in transit, and this website uses HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

08Your choices & deletion requests

  1. Optional statistics and crash reports

    Turn Settings → Data & privacy → Share usage data on or off. It is off until you enable it.

  2. Advertising choices

    Use Ad privacy options in Settings when available, and your device’s advertising controls. These choices are separate from optional Firebase sharing.

  3. Local progress and backups

    Use Settings → Reset progress to clear progress. For other local app data or device backups, use your operating system’s app storage and backup controls. Removing data can permanently remove your progress.

  4. Access or deletion requests

    Email dogukangamesdev@gmail.com with the subject “Goalbreaker privacy request” and describe what you would like us to do. You do not need to uninstall first.

Because Goalbreaker has no accounts, we may not be able to connect a report to you using a device model or approximate date alone. We will explain what we can identify, ask only for information reasonably needed to verify and handle your request, and use the relevant provider’s deletion tools where available. Information processed independently by an advertising provider may also require using that provider’s privacy controls. We respond within applicable legal time limits.

09Regional privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data; restrict or object to processing; or withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom & Switzerland

Where applicable, we rely on your consent for optional analytics, crash reporting and advertising processing that requires consent. We rely on legitimate interests to respond to support requests, secure our services and prevent abuse, and on legal obligations where processing is required by law. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and complain to your local data protection authority.

United States

Where state privacy laws apply, you may also have rights to opt out of targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information, and to appeal a decision about a privacy request. Advertising disclosures to partners can qualify as “sale” or “sharing” under some laws, even without a direct payment for the data. Use the advertising privacy options offered in the game, or contact us to exercise applicable rights or appeal a decision. We do not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

10Children’s privacy

Goalbreaker is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where local law requires a higher age for valid consent, that requirement applies.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or support, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11Privacy on this website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms or cookies of its own. Images and fonts are served with the site; visiting a page does not request fonts from Google Fonts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, as explained in its Pages documentation. External links and your email provider are governed by their own privacy practices.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available at this address with an updated effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or ask for renewed consent.
