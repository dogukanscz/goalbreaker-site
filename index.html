Draw the shot. Break the wall. Score.

Goalbreaker: Football Puzzle · Android (Google Play) · iOS later

Every level hides a goal behind a wall. Draw the path of your shot with one finger: a straight line is a straight kick, a curve is a curling ball. Bricks crack, wood splinters, glass shatters, ropes snap and the keeper dives. Find the one shot that opens the way, then put the ball in the net.

Plays anywhere. No account, no internet connection needed. Your progress stays on your device. Anonymous crash reports and play statistics can be sent to help us improve the game; you can turn them off in Settings.
