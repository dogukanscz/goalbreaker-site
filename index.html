How we make games

Fair, focused
and made to last.

01 /

Play comes first

Every level is played through and checked before it ships. If a puzzle is not fun to solve, it does not make the cut.

02 /

Fair rewards

No ads between levels. A rewarded video plays only when you choose an extra boost.

03 /

Your data, your choice

No account to create. Play statistics and crash reports are optional and stay off until you turn them on.
