Draw the shot. Break the wall. Score.

A football puzzle for your phone. Every level puts a wall between you and the goal; you have a few balls and one finger to find the way through.

Android, coming sooniPhone, coming soon

How it plays

Trace a line and the ball follows it: straight for a straight kick, bent for a curling one. Bricks crack, wood splinters, glass shatters, ropes give way and the keeper dives.

What's inside

125 levels in five chapters, side and daily walls, three stars per level for the sharpest solutions, and a wardrobe of kits, balls, trails and goal celebrations.

No strings

No account and nothing to sign up for. The game works offline and your progress stays on your phone. Ads only play when you choose to watch one.

Who makes it

Goalbreaker is made by Talvero Games, a one-person studio run by Doğukan Saçmaözü in Türkiye. Questions, bugs and ideas are welcome on the support page. How the game handles data is written out in plain words in the privacy policy (Türkçe).
