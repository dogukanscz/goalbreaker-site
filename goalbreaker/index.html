How to play

Every wall is
a new puzzle.

Every level puts a wall between you and the goal. Football instinct meets a little creative destruction. It’s easy to take a shot. Finding the perfect one is where the fun starts.

01

Find your angle

Draw a straight shot or bend your line into a curve. Read the obstacles and work out your way to the goal.

02

Bring it down

Break through wood, crack the bricks, shatter glass and send structures tumbling. Different walls need different ideas.

03

Make it count

Beat the keeper, chase three stars and come back for a sharper solution. A limited supply of balls makes every decision matter.

Goalbreaker FAQ

Before
kick-off.

What kind of game is Goalbreaker?

A football (soccer) puzzle game for Android. Each level puts a wall between you and the goal: draw your shot with one finger, bend it around obstacles and bring the wall down to score.

How many levels are there?

150 main levels across six chapters, with side roads and daily walls on top.

When can I play it?

Goalbreaker is coming soon to Google Play for Android. Follow @goalbreakergame on Instagram to hear when it launches.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Levels, unlocks and your progress work offline. Optional rewarded videos need an internet connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads between levels. A rewarded video plays only when you choose one for an extra boost or an in-game reward, and the current version has no in-app purchases. Support has the details.

Do I need an account?

No account to create. Your progress is stored on your device.

Which languages does it support?

The game is in English and Turkish.

Who makes Goalbreaker?

Doğukan Saçmaözü, the independent developer behind Talvero Games in Türkiye.
