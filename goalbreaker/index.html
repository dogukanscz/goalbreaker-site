How to play

The goal is
behind a wall.

Each level has a wall between you and the goal. Draw a shot, see how the wall falls and try again with a better line.

01

Drawing the shot

Draw a straight line or a curve. A curved line makes the ball curl around obstacles.

02

Breaking the wall

Walls are built from wood, brick, metal and glass. The materials behave differently, so each wall needs a different shot.

03

Limited balls

You have a limited number of balls per level and sometimes a goalkeeper to beat. A level gives up to three stars, so you can replay it for a better result.

Goalbreaker FAQ

Common
questions.

What kind of game is Goalbreaker?

A football (soccer) puzzle game for iPhone and Android. Each level puts a wall between you and the goal: draw your shot with one finger, bend it around obstacles and bring the wall down to score.

How many levels are there?

175 main levels in seven chapters. Smash & Score, a second mode, has 75 levels where you tap to shoot and clear a platform. There are also side roads, a daily wall and a weekly leaderboard.

Where can I get it?

Goalbreaker is out for iPhone and Android: download it on the App Store or get it on Google Play. News and updates are on Instagram at @goalbreakergame.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Levels, unlocks and your progress work offline. Ads need an internet connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

A rewarded video plays only when you choose one for an extra boost or an in-game reward. From version 1.0.6, a full-screen ad can also appear when you leave the results of a won level, at most once every five won levels and never during a level. From version 1.1.0 on Android and version 1.2.1 on iOS, optional one-time purchases remove ads. Support has the details.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up is needed to play. Your progress is stored on your device. The optional online leaderboard uses an anonymous player ID and a nickname you choose; see the leaderboard rules.

Which languages does it support?

The game is in English and Turkish.

Who makes Goalbreaker?

Talvero Games, the independent studio of Doğukan Saçmaözü in Türkiye.
