Questions about the ranking? ↗

These rules apply to the RANKING leaderboard in Goalbreaker version 1.3.0 and later. Before you can type a nickname, the game asks you to tick “I accept the ranking rules”; the box starts unticked. By ticking it you agree to these rules: they are the terms on which we run the leaderboard for you. How we handle leaderboard data is explained in the privacy policy and, in Turkish, in our information notice.

01Who can take part

  • Joining is optional and free. Players aged 16 and over can join from the ranking screen.
  • Players aged 13 to 15 can follow the tables but cannot join. The game sends nothing about them and shows them only nicknames we have reviewed; other nicknames appear as “Player”.
  • For players who answered that they are under 13, the leaderboard does not exist: the game does not show it and sends no leaderboard request.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the leaderboard is view-only for now: nobody there can join, the game sends nothing about the player, and nicknames are not shown: rows read “Player 1234”, a four-digit number your device derives only for display, which does not identify the player. The game decides this on your device from device signals only: on Android, the mobile network’s country, then the SIM card’s country, then the device’s region setting; on iPhone, the device’s region setting. If any available signal names one of these countries, or no signal is available, the leaderboard is view-only. The signals are kept in memory only, never stored and never sent.
  • You need an internet connection to see the tables and to send scores. The rest of the game works offline as before.
  • Joining creates an anonymous ranking identity for this game. It needs no email address or password.

02Your nickname

  • 3 to 16 characters: letters A–Z, Turkish letters (Ç Ğ İ Ö Ş Ü ç ğ ı ö ş ü) and digits 0–9. A space, dot, underscore or hyphen may appear between characters, one at a time, but not at the start or end.
  • Nicknames are not unique: other players may use the same one.
  • You can change your nickname once a day (24 hours after the last change).
  • Your nickname is shown to everyone. Do not use your full real name, and never include a phone number, address, email address, link or social media handle.

Not allowed, with zero tolerance:

  • insults, hate or harassment aimed at a person or a group, including because of origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability;
  • sexual or obscene words, including disguised spellings;
  • references to drugs, violence, terrorism or crime;
  • pretending to be another person, a club, a brand, Goalbreaker, Talvero Games or a moderator;
  • contact details, links or advertising;
  • anything illegal.

03How scores are counted

There are two periods, THIS WEEK and ALL TIME, and three tables in each: STARS, LEVEL and TROPHIES. Coins and gems do not count for the leaderboard in any way.

This week

  • A week runs from Monday 00:00 to the next Monday 00:00, Türkiye time (Sunday 21:00 to Sunday 21:00 UTC). Every weekly table starts again from zero when the week begins.
  • Stars: for each level you play this week, your best star result this week, added up over all levels and modes (at most 663). Replaying a level does not add to it unless you beat your best for the week.
  • Level: the number of different levels in which you scored a goal this week, in any mode (at most 221).
  • Trophies: your trophy points (KP) this week, from 0 to 400. Six weekly trophies (Matchweek, First Touch, Three-Star Week, Big Games, Every Day on the Pitch and Smash Week) each have bronze, silver and gold steps, worth up to 60 points per trophy; when all six reach gold, the Perfect Week bonus adds 40 more. Most of them count different levels you win this week, in any mode, so replaying a level adds nothing; Every Day on the Pitch counts the days on which you win the daily wall. Each trophy can advance only so far in one day, and all six start again from zero every Monday.

All time

  • Stars: your best result in each level, added up.
  • Level: the highest main campaign level you have won (1 to 150).
  • Trophies: the points of your permanent collection trophies (up to 1,190), plus the points of every weekly trophy week you have finished, plus this week’s. Collection trophies you won before version 1.3.0 add their points once to the all-time table and nothing to a weekly one.

Only play without help counts for trophies

A win does not move your trophies if you used a continue ball, a hint, a power shot from your stock, the extra star from a rewarded video, or a TNT ball or fire ball in that attempt. The win still gives you everything else the game gives. Purchases, coins, gems and ads never add trophy points.

For every table

  • Rank depends only on your play. There is no draw.
  • If two players have the same score, the one who reached it first ranks higher.
  • Star results with help count with the stars the game gave you, including the extra star from a rewarded video.
  • Resetting your progress does not lower your all-time scores.
  • Your game sends your scores when you leave the results of a won level (at most about once a minute), when you open the ranking and when it starts with an unsent update. A large jump in stars or levels reaches the table in steps over the next sends, usually within about an hour of play. The server refuses scores above what play can produce in the time since the last send.
  • Play that finished before the week closed but was not yet sent can still reach that week’s tables for up to 3 hours after the close, if you were already on them.
  • Your exact rank is shown up to 1,000; after that it shows “1000+”.

04Weekly rewards

  • Taking part is free. No purchase is needed to join or to win.
  • The top 10 of each of the three weekly tables (stars, level and trophies) receive the rewards below. The all-time tables give no rewards.
  • 1st: 30 gems and 500 coins, and a crown next to your name for one week
  • 2nd–3rd: 15 gems and 250 coins
  • 4th–10th: 5 gems and 100 coins
  • Rewards can be collected in the game from Monday 12:00 Türkiye time (09:00 UTC) after the week closes, until 14 days after the close. Open the game online in that period; uncollected rewards expire.
  • Only joined players aged 16 and over can win. Before rewards can be collected, we may check the top of each table and remove scores that break these rules. A banned identity receives no rewards.
  • Rewards are in-game coins and gems only. They have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods or anything else of value.
  • Organiser: Talvero Games, Türkiye (contact in section 9). Apple and Google are not sponsors of the leaderboard or its rewards and are not involved in them in any way.
  • These rules and the reward amounts are announced on this page. Changes are announced here before they apply. Rewards already paid stay in your game. Void where prohibited by law.

05Reporting and hiding

  • Press and hold another player’s row on the ranking screen, or tap its “…” button, to hide or report that player. Reporting in the game is for players aged 16 and over who have joined; hiding works for everyone who sees names.
  • A report covers the player, not only the nickname. Choose one of four reasons: offensive or inappropriate name; pretending to be someone else; cheating or impossible score; something else. The reported player is not told who reported them. You can send up to 10 reports a day and report each player once.
  • Everyone else, for example players aged 13 to 15 or players who have not joined, can report a player by email (section 9). The game’s “Report by e-mail” button opens a message with the table, the rank, the nickname and the date.
  • Hide removes a player from your own view. It is stored only on your device; nobody else is told. Settings → Show hidden players brings them all back.

06How names are checked, and who decides

  • When you set a nickname, the game and the server check it automatically against the character rules and a list of blocked words. A blocked name is refused at once; a name that passes appears straight away. No filter catches everything.
  • Every other decision is made by a person, the developer: renaming a nickname and locking it so it cannot be changed back, removing a player from a week’s tables, and banning a ranking identity, which stops it from sending scores and from receiving rewards.
  • We aim to review reports within 24 hours. When we cannot review them for a longer time, we pause the leaderboard.
  • When we act on your entry, the game tells you what was done, which rule it broke (offensive or inappropriate name; pretending to be someone else; cheating or impossible score; something else) and that a person decided.
  • To contest a decision, email us (section 9) with your ranking code from Settings and why you think it is wrong. A person reads it and answers.
  • Every decision needs a reason code. We keep a short log of each decision (a short code, the action, the reason code and the date) for 12 months, and no free-text notes about players.

07Cheating

Editing save files, using scripts or modified versions of the game, exploiting bugs, or using several identities to raise a score or to flood reports is cheating. We remove cheating scores and may ban the identity. Reinstalling the game does not lift a ban: a new identity that breaks the rules is banned too.

08Leaving and deleting your data

Goalbreaker (Talvero Games): how to delete your ranking account and data.

In the game

  • Open Settings → Leave the ranking and delete my data (also in the help sheet behind the ? button on the ranking screen). It is the one way to leave, and it always deletes your ranking data. It is available whenever your game holds a ranking identity, whatever your age range, and also while the leaderboard is paused.
  • The game first asks you to confirm and tells you what is removed. Online it takes a few moments. Offline, the game keeps trying by itself and tells you when the deletion has finished. In the rare case that it cannot finish, it tells you so and asks you to email us your ranking code; we then finish the deletion.
  • Unclaimed rewards are lost. Your progress in the game stays. If you join again later, you start with a new identity.
  • If the age answer stored on your device is below 16 and the game still holds a ranking identity (for example after restoring a backup and answering the age question again), the game deletes that identity’s ranking data by itself after the launch privacy steps. Players aged 13 to 15 are told once.

By email, from outside the game

  • While your game holds a ranking identity, Settings shows your RANKING CODE under the delete control. It has two parts: a short part that lets us find your entries, and a secret part that only your device shows. Tap it to copy it. Do not share it with anyone but us.
  • To ask us to delete your ranking data without the game, for example after uninstalling it, email dogukangamesdev@gmail.com with the subject “Goalbreaker privacy request” and your whole ranking code. The secret part shows that the request comes from you.
  • If your game lost an earlier ranking identity, or its entries could not be removed, Settings → Earlier ranking codes keeps that identity’s full code (up to three), so you can still ask us to delete that data. You can forget these codes there; that removes them from your device only.
  • If you no longer have the code, for example after reinstalling without a backup, write your nickname instead. If we cannot confirm that the entries are yours, we replace the nickname with a neutral one and do not delete the scores: we do not delete anyone’s scores at another person’s request.
  • We may ask for more information to confirm a request. We answer every request within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what stays for how long

  • At once: your entries leave every table and your nickname and scores are removed; then your anonymous ranking identity is deleted.
  • Empty markers that keep a deleted entry from coming back: removed within 30 days, together with the reports you filed and your report counter. If Google does not let the game delete the empty identity at that moment, we delete it within the same 30 days.
  • Reports other players filed about you: deleted when we close them, at the latest after 90 days. A deleted identity can no longer file reports, and other players’ report counters that named you are blanked.
  • A ban record, if you were banned: while the ban applies.
  • Our moderation and deletion log (a short code, the action, the reason code and the date): 12 months.
  • Google, which runs the database for us, removes deleted data from its backups within 180 days.

09Contact and reports

dogukangamesdev@gmail.com is our single contact point for the leaderboard: questions, reports of a nickname (including content you believe is illegal), contesting a moderation decision and deletion requests. Players, people who do not play and authorities can all use it. Write in English or Turkish; a person reads every message and answers. To report a nickname, please include the table, the rank, the nickname, the date and why you are reporting it.

In the game, the ? button on the ranking screen opens Help and contact: this address, these rules, the reward rules, the help page and an e-mail button.

Talvero Games · Doğukan Saçmaözü, Türkiye · dogukangamesdev@gmail.com. If you need a postal address for a written application, ask at the same address and we will send it.
